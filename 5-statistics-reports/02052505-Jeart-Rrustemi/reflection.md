# Personal Reflection

## What surprised me the most?

While looking into the Software Engineering related AI statistics and industry reports, I noticed how Artificial Intelligence is rapidly integrating into the various facets of modern development. Prior to this study, I was aware that certain AI tools, including coding assistants, were developed, but I did not realize how mature and pervasive their usage by developers and companies really is.

Upon studying the reports, the most shocking revelation for me was the rapid and widespread adoption of AI tools by software engineers. The majority of the reports indicated that developers use AI for code generation, debugging, documentation, testing, and overall productivity enhancement. The investment in AI by large corporations is an indicator of the potential reduction in development time and the improvements in efficiency.

## What concerns me about AI in software engineering?

Beyond the rapid evolution of AI, a few concerns began to form. Speed is one of the advantages of AI, including the ability to write code. However, that code is not always correct or secure or even the best answer. Thus, developers cannot work with such code and solutions. Critical thinking is a crucial element of software development, and overreliance on AI could lead to a loss of that crucial skill.

## Which skills do I think will remain important in the AI era?

Additionally, I established that software development is more than just coding, and the future of software development will entail collaboration with teams and with AI in a flexible and adaptable manner, as well as having good communication and creativity in decision making. AI may perform certain tasks, but creatively and critically thinking and making informed decisions while working together with AI is still of the utmost importance and will not be replaced.

## How did my opinion change after completing this research?

After completing this research, my opinion about AI in software engineering changed significantly. Initially, I believed AI could eventually replace many programming jobs entirely. However, after analyzing industry reports and developer statistics, I now understand that AI is more likely to transform the role of software engineers rather than eliminate it. Engineers who learn how to properly use AI tools while still maintaining strong technical foundations will likely have a major advantage in the future job market.

## How will I personally adapt in the future?

Personally, I think continuous learning will become one of the most important responsibilities for future software engineers. Technology evolves rapidly, and developers must adapt to new tools, workflows, and industry expectations. I believe it is important for me to continue improving not only my programming skills, but also my problem-solving abilities, communication skills, and understanding of AI-assisted development. Overall, this research helped me better understand both the opportunities and challenges created by AI, and it gave me a clearer perspective about the future of software engineering.
